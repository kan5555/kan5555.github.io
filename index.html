About

A little about me

Who I am

I'm a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Sun Research Group, Department of Materials Science and Engineering, University of Michigan. I completed my Ph.D. at Kyoto University in 2026 as a JSPS Research Fellow (DC1).

Research focus

My work sits at the intersection of solid-state chemistry, materials science and condensed-matter physics. So far I have worked on polar metals, mixed-anion compounds, and anion-redox oxides.

Research

What I'm working on

01

High-Pressure Synthesis

Designing and discovering new oxides and mixed-anion compounds through high-pressure routes that are inaccessible at ambient conditions.

High pressure Oxides & mixed-anion compounds
02

Topochemical Reaction

Building metastable mixed-anion compounds through topochemical reactions, including anion exchange, the creation of anion vacancies, and the introduction of anion intercalation.

Topochemistry Metastable phases
03

Structure to Property Relationship

Linking atomic-scale motifs such as anion ordering, polar distortions, and lattice softening to bulk physical properties such as magnetism, transport, and anion-redox electrochemistry.

Structural chemistry Functional materials
